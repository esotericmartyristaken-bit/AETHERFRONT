Aetherfront - Windows KSP 1.12.5

1. Close KSP. Extract the starter ZIP into a licensed KSP 1.12.5 game folder,
   beside KSP_x64.exe. Merge the folders. Keep Squad and any DLC you own.
2. Launch KSP. At the main menu, open Aetherfront, enter your name, server IP
   and port, choose Regular or Potato, then Connect.
3. Review the listed changes in game. Approve removal of unrelated mods only
   if you want them permanently deleted, without a removed-mod backup. Cancel
   to keep your installed files. Approval alone does not begin the commit.
4. Download, preparation and verification progress stays in the game window.
   Wait for the explicit Restart & apply button. KSP stays open until you
   choose that button; Cancel keeps the current installation.
5. Click Restart & apply once when ready. This authorizes the prepared client
   and selected pack update together. KSP closes normally; a hidden helper
   commits and verifies the files, then relaunches KSP. No external installation
   window opens. Do not force-close KSP or the helper during this commit, and
   do not start another KSP instance while waiting for the verified relaunch.

Connect downloads only missing or changed packages. Verified downloads remain
cached after interruption. If files already match, Connect joins without an
installation restart. Do not close KSP during preparation unless you first
cancel and wait for cancellation to finish.

One legacy transition: an installation still running RC24 or earlier must first
migrate using its OLD updater code. That migration may show the old external
completion window or use its old restart behavior. Follow that window's
instructions and keep KSP closed while it installs. The new in-game button and
hidden-helper flow apply once the RC25 client is running; they cannot change
an updater already loaded by the old client.

Regular and Potato share gameplay content. Potato removes optional graphics
and sound effects and uses low graphics settings. Potato uses the official
Kcalbeloh 4K textures; Regular uses 8K. Height and biome maps are identical.
Your profile choice is saved; change it from the main menu, then Connect.

Saves, identity and controls are preserved. Replaced managed files retain
failure recovery under APF/.transaction-backup. Approved removed mods are
permanently deleted and are NOT included in that recovery. Returning to Regular
restores the graphics settings saved before Potato mode. Never share your
player identity or private keys.

If preparation fails, read the in-game error. If the hidden commit fails, KSP
is not relaunched. Do not retry blindly or delete recovery data. Retain
APF/APFUpdater.log, APF/LastInstallResult.json and recovery data for diagnosis.
Only a complete LastInstallResult.json entry means every helper stage finished;
a pack transaction entry alone does not include the final graphics settings.

Only servers matching the starter's trusted certificate and signing key can
provide updates. Typing another IP does not bypass these checks.
